## Why This GitHub Exists

This is my **digital playground**. It's where I process ideas, follow my curiosity, and build things just to understand how they work — platforms, infrastructure, observability, and AI systems.

It's also my **classroom**. Everything here is personal work, built on my own time, to learn and to teach. I share it openly so others can learn with me.

## Who I Help

I mentor people who want to **break into IT or grow in it**, especially:

- 🌍 **Immigrants in the USA and Canada** starting or restarting a tech career in a new country.
- 🚀 **IT professionals** who want a sharper edge in Cloud, DevOps, SRE, and AI/ML so they can grow to the next level.

My goal is simple: turn *"This is too hard"* into *"I can do this."*
